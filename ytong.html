Ytong
Catalog

Visual Direction | Catalog Design | Advertising·IDEA Studio·2025

Ytong is one of the oldest and most recognized brands in Israel's construction industry, and its catalog for infrastructure and landscape development (paving, fences, stairs and retaining walls) is the main working tool for architects, landscape architects and contractors. Over the years the catalog became cluttered. The challenge was to refresh a catalog of more than 180 pages without losing the identity the brand built over decades, with the logo and the yellow color palette staying exactly as they are.

The solution was restraint: yellow remains the brand color but shifts from a dominant color to a precise accent for the chapter tab, a thin line and page numbering, repeated consistently throughout the catalog. The pages were decluttered and given room to breathe, so large photos from real projects, each with a caption, take the lead and show the product in the field. A table of contents with a clear hierarchy of chapters and subcategories makes navigation easier. When the design steps back, the product and the brand come out stronger.
