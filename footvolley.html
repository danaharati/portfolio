Team Águia
Footvolley Club

Brand Identity | Visual System·IDEA Studio·2026

Footvolley is a Brazilian beach sport that many people watch from a distance, without knowing how to start or where they belong. When an international club comes to Israel, its brand needs to feel like a place people want to be, not just another logo.

I designed a complete visual identity for Team Águia, a Brazilian footvolley club led by Maor Haas and Israel national team players. It brings the Brazilian beach to Tel Aviv. The green-and-yellow palette, the language of professional sport and the free-spirited energy carry through to every touchpoint.

Around the logo, I built a full collection of branded gear: bottles, sweat and beach towels, cooler bags, umbrellas, tents and beach chairs, plus caps, hoodies and pins. I branded the court too, so the beach itself becomes part of the brand. The social posts complete the picture, with photos of players in motion and bold typography that looks like the world of professional sport. The result is a brand that feels both professional and warm, and looks consistent everywhere you meet it.
